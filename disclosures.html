Coaching and community discussions are intended to help self-directed traders examine and improve their own processes.

Trading Risk

Trading securities, options, futures, foreign exchange, digital assets, and other financial instruments involves substantial risk. Losses can occur quickly and may include the entire amount committed. Certain leveraged products can expose a trader to losses greater than the initial amount deposited.

Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose. Consider your objectives, experience, and financial circumstances, and seek advice from appropriately licensed professionals when needed.
